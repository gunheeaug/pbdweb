What's Gather

Gather is classmate networking for MBA and master’s students — not dating. Discover peers at your school, match on shared interests, chat, and make plans after class. more

Preview

Gather helps MBA classmates meet in real life. Discover peers at your grad school, match on shared interests, and make plans in minutes — lunch after class, coffee between sessions, or a quick intro — without weeks of awkward outreach. Features: 🎓 School Discover: Swipe through classmates at your program and see school, career path, and interests. ✨ Mutual Matches: When you both want to connect, jump straight into a conversation. 💬 Chat to Gather: Plan lunch, coffee, or intros without losing the thread in a crowded group chat. 📝 Private Notes: Keep personal follow-ups on people you meet — what you talked about and what’s next. 👤 MBA Profile: Share school, program, interests, and a short bio so the right people find you first.

🎓 Built for MBA classmates.

Gather is professional classmate networking for graduate business school — swipe to connect, match on mutual interest, and gather after class.

🤝 Not a dating app.

Profiles center on school, program, career path, and what you want to gather for — so networking stays intentional and useful.
