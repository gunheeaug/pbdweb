Crèma Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Last Updated: February 1, 2026
Company: PBD LLC
Contact Email: hello@pbd.team

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use Crèma (the “Service”).

1) Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide, including:

  • Account information: name, username, email, password (stored hashed), profile photo
  • Profile information: bio, coffee preferences, experience/work history, skills, certifications, favorite cafés, links, and other details you choose to add
  • User Content: posts, photos, captions, comments, reactions, messages, and other content you upload or submit
  • Support and communications: messages to support, survey responses, and feedback

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect certain information when you use the Service, including:

  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system, app version, language, time zone, IP address, device identifiers (as permitted), and diagnostics
  • Usage data: screens viewed, interactions (likes/comments/follows/connections/shares), session timestamps, feature usage, and log data
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs and performance metrics to improve reliability

1.3 Location Data

Crèma supports location features, such as tagging cafés or places, which are essential for discovering the coffee community around you.

  • Precise location (optional): collected only if you enable location permissions and use location-enabled features like "Check-in" or "Find nearby cafes"
  • Approximate location: may be inferred from your IP address

1.4 Contacts (Optional)

If you choose to connect your contacts to find friends and fellow baristas on Crèma, we may process contact identifiers solely to provide this feature. You can revoke access at any time via your device settings.

2) How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate the Service (profiles, coffee feeds, posting, messaging, professional networking)
  • personalize content and recommendations (e.g., suggested baristas to follow, relevant coffee posts)
  • enable location tagging and place-related features
  • maintain safety, security, and integrity (detect spam, fraud, and abuse)
  • communicate with you (service announcements, security alerts, support responses)
  • analyze and improve the Service (debugging, analytics, feature performance)
  • enforce our Terms and comply with legal obligations

3) How We Share Information

3.1 With Other Users and the Public

Crèma is a social platform. Information you share may be visible to others depending on the Service design and your settings, including your coffee experience, preferences, and posts.

3.2 With Service Providers

We share information with vendors that help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, storage, analytics). Service providers may access information only as needed to perform services for us and must protect it.

3.3 For Legal, Safety, and Enforcement

We may disclose information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect safety, or enforce our Terms.

4) Your Choices and Controls

You can access and update your profile, delete posts, manage notifications, and disable location permissions through the app or device settings. You can request account deletion by emailing hello@pbd.team.

5) Data Retention

We retain information as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, and maintain security. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify personal information within a reasonable period.

6) Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. However, no system is completely secure. Please protect your account and use a strong password.

7) International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.
