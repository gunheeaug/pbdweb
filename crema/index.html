What's Crèma

Crèma is the professional and social home for the coffee world. Whether you're a barista sharing latte art, a roaster showcasing new beans, or a coffee lover looking for your next favorite cafe, Crèma is where the community gathers. more

Preview

Crèma is a social space specifically designed for the coffee enthusiasts. It's a dedicated space where coffee lovers, cafe regulars, baristas, pastry chefs, and coffee roasters interact, gather, and share their thoughts and coffee experiences. From professional networking for baristas to discovery for coffee aficionados, Crèma brings the entire coffee ecosystem together in one immersive digital experience. Features: ☕ Professional Profiles: Showcase your barista skills, certifications, and work history. 📸 Coffee Feed: Share your latest brew, latte art, or cafe visit with a community that appreciates the details. 📍 Cafe Discovery: Tag and explore cafes, roasteries, and hidden gems. 🤝 Community Connections: Follow your favorite baristas and roasters to stay updated on their latest creations. 💬 Expert Discussions: Engage in conversations about brewing techniques, bean origins, and cafe culture.

☕ The Coffee World, Connected.

Crèma is more than just an app; it's a digital third place. We've built a professional and social network that understands the nuances of the coffee industry—from the precision of a perfect pull to the warmth of a local cafe community.

👨‍🍳 For Professionals.

Baristas, roasters, and chefs can build their professional identity, share their craft, and connect with peers and employers in the industry.

❤️ For Lovers.

Coffee enthusiasts and regulars can discover new spots, learn from experts, and share their daily coffee journey with a community that shares their passion.
